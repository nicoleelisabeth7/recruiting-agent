# Product Marketing Manager Scoring Rubric

## Overview
This rubric evaluates candidates for product marketing manager roles across 5 dimensions. Each dimension is scored 0–4, for a maximum total of 20 points.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. PMM Title Depth (0–4)
**Definition:** Clean, consecutive product marketing title history with demonstrated seniority progression.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | 7+ years clean Sr./Lead PMM or equivalent titles; no unexplained gaps; clear progression through mid to senior levels |
| 3 | 5–7 years PMM title history; may include 1 gap or role transition; mostly focused on PMM |
| 2 | 3–5 years PMM title history OR titles are ambiguous (e.g., "GTM Leader" without explicit PMM); mixed adjacent roles |
| 1 | <3 years PMM title OR majority of career in adjacent marketing (field, partner, demand gen, digital) with 1–2 PMM roles |
| 0 | No PMM title history; career in non-marketing or unrelated marketing functions |

**Notes:**
- "Senior Product Marketing Manager" and "Lead Product Marketing Manager" both count as Sr. level
- Adjacent titles (Content Marketing Manager, Integrated Marketing, Growth Marketing) do NOT count toward PMM tenure
- Short stints (<12 months) in PMM role count but flag tenure pattern concerns

---

### 2. Enterprise B2B SaaS GTM (0–4)
**Definition:** Proven ability to launch, position, and enable sales in enterprise SaaS environments with measurable pipeline/revenue impact.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Led GTM for 3+ enterprise SaaS product launches; demonstrated pipeline/revenue impact ($5M+ pipeline, 10%+ win rate uplift, or 100+ enterprise customer acquisition); managed high-complexity buyer journeys |
| 3 | Led GTM for 1–2 enterprise SaaS launches; measurable sales enablement impact; experience with enterprise sales motions OR experience at $50M+ funded/revenue companies |
| 2 | B2B SaaS experience but limited launch ownership; demand generation focus OR mid-market SaaS; GTM support vs. ownership |
| 1 | Mixed B2B/B2C; SMB/startup SaaS only; or B2B but non-SaaS (services, hardware) |
| 0 | No B2B SaaS experience; primarily B2C, consumer, or non-marketing roles |

**Notes:**
- Cloud/data/analytics platforms count as enterprise SaaS if buyer is enterprise (CIO/CTO) and ASP >$50k
- Procurement/sourcing platforms are SaaS but scored separately for Domain Fit
- Multi-launch leadership beats single launch mastery at this score level

---

### 3. AI/Agentic Product Marketing (0–4)
**Definition:** Experience marketing AI-powered, complex, or emerging capability products; fluency with AI tools in PMM workflows.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Led GTM for 2+ AI/agentic/complex capability products; translated technical AI capabilities into buyer narratives; demonstrated deep fluency with LLMs and AI tools in PMM execution (research, positioning, content) |
| 3 | Led GTM for 1 AI/emerging-tech product OR demonstrated strong AI tool fluency (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) in PMM workflows; some positioning experience with technical complexity |
| 2 | Exposure to AI products (sold them, supported launches) OR basic AI tool proficiency mentioned; not core to role |
| 1 | Minimal AI product experience; mentions AI tools but no demonstrated depth or usage |
| 0 | No AI product experience; no AI tool proficiency |

**Notes:**
- "AI-native" means actively using LLMs for drafting, research, competitive analysis, persona development
- Agentic products = agents, orchestration, automation platforms, workflow automation
- AI tool proliferation in marketing tech counts for credit, but product marketing AI focus is weighted higher

---

### 4. Positioning & Sales Enablement (0–4)
**Definition:** Demonstrated ability to craft positioning, build sales assets (battlecards, talk tracks, objection handling), and enable revenue teams.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Built positioning frameworks from scratch; created 5+ sales enablement assets (battlecards, objection handling, exec decks, playbooks); trained sales teams; measured sales lift from enablement |
| 3 | Developed positioning for 2+ product lines; created 3–5 sales assets; sales team adoption/feedback mentioned; some measurement of impact |
| 2 | Positioning support (helped refine, adapted existing); created 1–2 sales assets; limited evidence of sales team engagement |
| 1 | Minimal positioning work; content creation vs. true messaging strategy; sales enablement mentioned but minimal detail |
| 0 | No positioning or sales enablement experience |

**Notes:**
- "Sales enablement" includes training, decks, one-pagers, release notes, demo scripts, case studies used by sales
- Measurement could be deal velocity, win rate, sales confidence, cycle time, or explicit feedback
- Launch readiness and campaign support count if they include sales motion design

---

### 5. Domain Fit: Procurement/Supply Chain/Sourcing (0–4)
**Definition:** Direct experience marketing to procurement leaders (CPOs), sourcing platforms, supply chain software, or procurement operations workflows.

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Led PMM for procurement/sourcing/supply chain platform; marketed to CPOs, procurement leaders, or sourcing teams; familiar with tools (Coupa, Ariba, Workday Sourcing) or workflows |
| 3 | 2+ years marketing to procurement/sourcing buyers; some domain terminology/category knowledge; adjacent platforms (financial operations, source-to-pay) |
| 2 | Exposure to procurement/sourcing (customer base, adjacent product line); basic familiarity with buyer pain points |
| 1 | Minimal procurement experience; adjacent domains (operations, finance, supply chain from non-procurement angle) |
| 0 | No procurement, sourcing, or supply chain background |

**Notes:**
- Supply chain ≠ procurement (procurement is subset of source-to-pay; supply chain is broader logistics/operations)
- Finance operations, spend management, financial automation count as adjacent (score: 1–2)
- Sourcing optimization or category strategy expertise is valuable but not core PMM domain (score: 1–2)
- No procurement background is NOT disqualifying for the role, but scores lower on this dimension

---

## Scoring Rules

1. **Score each dimension independently** — do not carry over gaps from one dimension to another
2. **Total = sum of 5 dimension scores (max 20)**
3. **Tiering:**
   - **Tier 1 (15–20):** Shortlist candidates — present with full score table, role list, written analysis
   - **Tier 2 (11–14):** Secondary candidates — present with score table and brief analysis
   - **Tier 3 (0–10):** Not recommended — name only, one-sentence disqualifier
4. **Ivy League Flag:** Always surface, regardless of score, with explicit note on fit gap if score <13
5. **Red Flags (document explicitly):**
   - PMM tenure <5 years clean history → flag as "Junior"
   - All roles <2 years tenure → flag as "High Tenure Churn"
   - Employment dates in future or gaps >1 year → flag as "Verify Status"
   - No enterprise SaaS GTM → flag as "SMB/Startup Only"
